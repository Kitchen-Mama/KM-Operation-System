CHANGE
The Site Inventory Method picker can read carrier identities and domestic
service declarations from shipment_route_templates, separately from quotes
and lead times. Disabled SRT-DOM templates declare capability only for a
zero-active-card carrier, as already supported by server 12_. They stay disabled.
No route activation, price, currency, booking, customs waiver or ETA is implied.
An active, unique, same-company overseas 3PL and an exact physical-country lane
are required. A template naming EU or a blank country grants no domestic lane.
The implementation is shared by all stations; carrier coverage is not widened.
WFS physical destinations remain paused; Walmart self-fulfilled is not disabled.

METHOD MAPPING (canonical tokens, not display labels)
Method truck + Last Mile parcel = SPD.
Method truck + Last Mile truck = LTL.
BestWay: US LTL. USPS / UPS / FedEx: configured US SPD only.
Amazon: configured US / CA / UK / DE / AU / JP / FR domestic SPD and LTL.
Carrier selection remains deferred to Shipping Plan Review.
