Site Priority

Shared-pool allocation priority per marketplace site. Higher wins. A site with no priority is Missing, which is not the same as 0 — 0 is a valid lowest priority that still takes a share. Sites are listed highest weight first, within their own company; sites at the same weight are ordered by name so the list does not shuffle on its own.

Priority is read by the overseas shared-pool allocator. A Missing priority is refused by the allocator rather than treated as 0, so a site left blank here is not silently ranked last — it is unresolved and its allocation will not run.
A higher weight is compared earlier when sites are ranked, and increases a site's share only when supply is short. At or below 1 the share stops changing — 0, 0.5 and 1 all receive the same shortage multiplier, because the allocator clamps the multiplier at 1 — but they remain three different positions in the ordering.
A weight of 0 does not switch a site off. It is the lowest valid weight: the site still takes its share, and still receives the same multiplier as a site at 1. What 0 does is rank it last among its company. A site with no priority is a different thing again — it is unresolved, and the allocator refuses it rather than treating it as 0.

To reorder a site: press Space or Enter on its handle to lift it, ↑ ↓ to move it, Home or End for the ends, Space to drop, Esc to cancel. Dropping only proposes a weight — nothing is saved until you choose Save.
